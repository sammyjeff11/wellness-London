## Guidelines
- Keep the first email focused on accurate photography and listings, not a paid upsell.
- One outreach per operator where possible. Verify email recipients in a private contact system.
- Do not attach screenshots of images scraped from the venue site or imply their use has been cleared.
- Don't use follow-ups more than once without founder direction. Respect opt-outs immediately.
- No automatic sending; never send without explicit founder consent.
