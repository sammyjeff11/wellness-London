## Finding

## Writing standard

1. Lead with the service, location and access. Add the duration, price basis, inclusions and restrictions that distinguish the booking.
2. Attribute operator-published information. Do not turn operator marketing into an independently established fact.
3. Use first-hand atmosphere or quality observations only with a named visitor and visit date. Keep observations separate from facts and operator claims.
4. Specify what professional training, qualification or supervision actually means. Do not substitute one for another.
5. Health-effect claims need a source relevant to the exact intervention and outcome, plus its limitations. Otherwise remove the claim.
6. Preserve uncertainty. Do not turn missing information into either a promised facility or a claim that the facility is absent.
7. Remove a sentence if it adds no fact, useful distinction or concrete booking question. Avoid repeating generic advice where the page already explains it.
